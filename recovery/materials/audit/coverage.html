United States · all-sector disbursements

Every country-year in the database matches its original ForeignAssistance.gov country file in current U.S. dollars. A separately archived U.S. government sector file reports larger later-year totals. The table uses the same set of 250 eligible country codes in both files. Worldwide and regional amounts stay outside that country comparison. Both files include military assistance.

A blank database year means there is no observation. Positive and negative revisions, late reporting and scope changes can all affect a difference. The external sector extract ends in FY2025. Year-end reporting completeness is unverified.

Kenya · China Eximbank

Kenya’s National Treasury records a US$1.6 billion agreement for the Mombasa–Nairobi railway on May 11, 2014. It matches database commitment gcdf-31777. The creditor summary on page 1 reports US$44,721,731.99 and CNY 454,243,778.75 in drawdowns for FY2022/23. The PRC database contains no structured Kenyan payment row. The drawdowns cover the creditor’s portfolio. The register shows no FY drawdown on this particular railway facility on page 23.

China · national reporting scope

The State Council’s 2021 white paper describes national foreign assistance for 2013–18. It provides a period-level reference for grants, interest-free loans and concessional loans. The database also records commercial official finance and usually dates commitments. A percentage of China’s national aid spending covered by the database cannot be calculated from these measures. Where to investigate next

  1. Refresh U.S. FY2024–25. FY2024 has 52 recipients present only in the sector file. Begin with Israel and Ukraine for the largest FY2024 dollar revisions. Preserve the old release and reconcile fiscal-year totals and pooled geographic labels before replacement.
  2. Extract actual PRC payments. The database has eleven dated payment observations across four recipients. Start with Kenya’s Treasury register, retaining the original currency, fiscal period, creditor and loan identifier. Keep facility balances, repayments and cumulative disbursements in distinct fields.
  3. Recover outcome dates. All 4,737 MCC monitoring indicators lack an observation year. Of these, 3,089 have a numeric actual and another 210 have a text-only actual. Use dated compact monitoring workbooks to link indicator ID, reporting period and actual. A program’s approval year cannot date an outcome.
  4. Check private sponsor disclosures. The PPI release supplies equity amounts on 80 of 366 U.S. and 52 of 161 Chinese sponsor records. Prioritize financial-close filings and sponsor accounts. Keep project-wide costs separate from each sponsor’s equity.
How to read coverage

The 250 entries cover present-day ISO jurisdictions plus Kosovo. Some did not exist in earlier years. The grid tracks records held by each source. Grants, loans, commitments, payments and private projects have different reporting frames. An empty cell alone cannot establish zero activity, an omitted project or a percentage of all spending.

Funding uses the flow year and displays all retained rows, including formally excluded records. The cell detail gives summary-eligible counts. Results use the actual observation year. Undated records have their own column. The numeric disclosure rate uses the retained row count as its denominator. Reported zeroes are disclosed values. National trade context is shared across providers and appears once. The U.S. cross-check remaps the source’s CS-KM label to Kosovo and applies the current country registry to both vintages.
