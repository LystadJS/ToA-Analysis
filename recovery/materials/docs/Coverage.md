# Country and year completeness audit

The largest demonstrable U.S. gap is the FY2024 release vintage. The largest obstacle on the PRC side is the lack of payment observations. Country registration, presence of a project, disclosure of its value, and evidence that money was paid answer different questions.

## What to collect next

1. **Refresh the U.S. country and sector files together.** Israel’s FY2024 amount changes from $900,001 to $6,818,569,348. Ukraine’s changes from $388,395,711 to $6,509,549,285. Start with the largest paired changes, then review the 52 sector-only recipients and pooled allocations. Preserve both releases and the sign of each revision. Keep military assistance identifiable in briefing totals.
2. **Collect PRC cash flows from borrower registers.** The structured database holds eleven dated disbursement observations across four recipients. Use Kenya as a starting point and link by lender, facility identifier, currency and borrower fiscal period. Maintain separate fields for agreements, drawdowns, repayments and debt stocks.
3. **Resolve high-value commitment and private-equity disclosure gaps.** GCDF has disclosed dollars on 10,517 of 17,743 summary-eligible adjusted commitments (59.3%). CLG has 2,036 of 3,677 summary-eligible face-value commitments (55.4%). For infrastructure sponsors, 80 of 366 U.S. and 52 of 161 Chinese PPI rows disclose equity. The unknown dollar share cannot be inferred from these record percentages. Private philanthropy currently covers twelve reviewed U.S. grants. The overall population of private efforts is unknown.
4. **Date the measured outcomes.** All 4,737 MCC indicators lack an observation year. Of these, 3,089 have a numeric actual, 210 have a text-only actual and 1,438 have neither. Retrieve dated monitoring workbooks and join on indicator identifiers. Among the five PRC result observations, only one has an observation year. A newly dated outcome must still be assessed on its evaluation method before making an impact claim.

## Reading the visualizations

The interactive HTML covers all 250 current jurisdictions and 41 provider, source and measure combinations. The U.S. comparison covers 6,250 country–fiscal-year cells from FY2001–25. The source view spans 1946–2026 and includes an undated column. The printable PDF covers 2001–25 for all 250 entries on eight pages. Its left panel compares U.S. releases. Its right panel measures Chinese commitment amount disclosure, with a source break after 2021.

An observed numeric zero counts as disclosed. An absent row within an observed source year has unknown activity. A year with no source rows anywhere is separately marked. The date is the finance flow year, program year, survey year or result observation year, according to the selected series. Project approval and report publication years cannot supply a missing observation date. The current country registry includes territories and countries that did not exist in every historic year. Funding cells include all retained source rows and the interactive detail gives the summary-eligible count.

The CSV for source observations is sparse and contains recorded cells, including undated records. Use the HTML’s filtered export to generate a complete country-by-year rectangle with explicit absence states. The U.S. reconciliation CSV already includes all 250 entries in every year. Its paired difference is blank wherever either release lacks an observation. A country’s presence in either provider’s records cannot establish complete coverage of all its projects.
