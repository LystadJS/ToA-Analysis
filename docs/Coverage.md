## Reading the visualizations

The interactive HTML covers all 250 current jurisdictions and 48 provider, source and measure combinations. The U.S. comparison covers 6,250 country–fiscal-year cells from FY2001–25. The source view spans 1946–2026 and includes an undated column. The printable PDF covers 2001–25 for all 250 entries on eight pages. Its left panel compares U.S. releases. Its right panel measures Chinese commitment amount disclosure, with a source break after 2021.

An observed numeric zero counts as disclosed. An absent row within an observed source year has unknown activity. A year with no source rows anywhere is separately marked. The date is the finance flow year, program year, survey year or result observation year, according to the selected series. Project approval and report publication years cannot supply a missing observation date. The current country registry includes territories and countries that did not exist in every historic year. Funding cells include all retained source rows and the interactive detail gives the summary-eligible count.

The CSV for source observations is sparse and contains recorded cells, including undated records. Use the HTML’s filtered export to generate a complete country-by-year rectangle with explicit absence states. The U.S. reconciliation CSV already includes all 250 entries in every year. Its paired difference is blank wherever either release lacks an observation. A country’s presence in either provider’s records cannot establish complete coverage of all its projects.
