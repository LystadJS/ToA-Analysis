# What diplomats can cite

**Honduras offers direct evidence on a trade procedure.** The 2024 Feed the Future Interagency Report says agricultural inspections at Puerto Cortés moved to a paperless system with joint inspections. USAID assisted the Honduran customs and phytosanitary agencies. The report says processing time fell by half and annual savings exceeded $15 million. It gives neither the initial number of hours nor a comparison port. The $15 million is an annual savings estimate. The project contribution is undisclosed on that page. [Feed the Future, printed p. 20](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nejohnson2/usaid-feed-the-future/6f48bc14d154da79cf88faeb97bc3f1da1bcd0de/docs/annual-reports/USAID3017_FTF-2024-Annual-Report_121624508c.pdf#page=20)

These examples support a discussion of which procedures a partner wants to change and how it would measure benefits. They do not establish the prospective return to joining Trade over Aid. Country briefs include the full indicator extract so readers can examine adverse changes, missing observations and source coverage.
